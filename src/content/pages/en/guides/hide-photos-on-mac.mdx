## Limits and risks

- **Not separate encryption.** Locking the Hidden album stops someone who opens Photos on your unlocked Mac from browsing it. It does not change how the photos are stored.
- **Sync spreads the photos.** With iCloud Photos on, hidden images exist on every signed-in device and in iCloud. Anyone with your Apple Account credentials can reach them.
- **Exports and shares leak.** A hidden photo you send, AirDrop or export is a normal file afterwards.
- **FileVault matters.** Without FileVault, someone with physical access to the disk can read the library without logging in.
