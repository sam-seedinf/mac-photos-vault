## Which should you use?

- **Lost-laptop worry:** FileVault. Turn it on today.
- **A few sensitive folders:** an encrypted disk image.
- **Photos and files you open often:** a vault app, so you don't mount and eject.
- **Sending files to someone:** an AES-encrypted archive, with the password shared separately from the file.

## Limits and risks

- Encryption protects data only as well as your password and your recovery key. Losing both means losing the data.
- FileVault does nothing about malware or someone using your logged-in Mac.
- Encrypted copies don't replace backups. Back up the files, and encrypt the backup as well.
