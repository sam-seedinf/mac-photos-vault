## Limits and risks

- **Lose the password, lose the data.** Apple can't unlock a disk image for you.
- **Mounted means open.** While the image is mounted, anyone using your logged-in Mac can read it, and apps may index its contents.
- **The original stays unencrypted until you delete it.** On a Mac with FileVault off, deleted files can sometimes be recovered; turning on FileVault protects the disk as a whole.
- **Image-in-image workflow is manual.** You mount, work, and eject each time. Some apps struggle to use files on an image that isn't mounted yet.
- **Don't store the password where the image lives.** A saved keychain password opens the image automatically for anyone who can unlock your Mac.
