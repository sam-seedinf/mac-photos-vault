## How we compared them

We looked at five things:

1. **Encryption.** Are the photos encrypted at rest, and with what?
2. **Key handling.** Does the key stay on your Mac?
3. **Everyday use.** Can you browse and add photos without mounting or exporting?
4. **Platforms and sync.** Does it work on the devices you use?
5. **Price and openness.** Is it free, and can anyone inspect the code?

Prices change often, so confirm on each vendor's site. Entries marked "ours" are made by us.
