## What to look for in a Mac alternative

- Photos encrypted on disk, not only hidden.
- A key that stays on your device.
- Touch ID so you don't retype a password every time.
- A way to move photos in without exporting them one by one.

Vaultaire is a third-party name; it belongs to its owners. Check the vendor's site for its current features.
