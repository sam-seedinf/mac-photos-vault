## How to choose

- **Only hiding photos from family?** The Hidden album.
- **A folder of documents you rarely open?** An encrypted disk image.
- **Worried about theft or loss?** FileVault, always.
- **Private files you open daily?** A vault app, so locking and unlocking stays quick.

## Limits of this comparison

Secure Vault is not released. We list its planned behavior so you can compare it fairly, and we'll update this page once the beta ships.
